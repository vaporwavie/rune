- **`Toolbar::height` sets the band's height and `Toolbar::divider` draws a hairline on its bottom edge.** A `SplitView` gives the sidebar's title bar row the same height and centres the sidebar toggle in it. The hairline is drawn inside the band and takes no room. The default stays `TOOLBAR_HEIGHT` with no hairline; an app that changes the height moves the traffic lights in its `Chrome`.
